CityNav uses the SOG conversion published with Will Eastcott's splat-walk
(https://github.com/willeastcott/splat-walk), cropped to the playable segment
and thinned farther from the walking route. The application adds separate
approximate collision geometry, an aligned local coordinate frame and
navigation tasks.
